# Scope and goals

Source: https://example.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PRJ/pages/2002/Scope+and+goals · Updated: 2026-10-08

## Goals

1. Check in a visitor in under one minute.
2. Remove the paper log at the pilot site.
3. Export a daily visitor list for the front desk.

## Out of scope

- Badge printing
- Contractor timekeeping
- Other offices during the pilot
