Flint Markdown Export

Turn a Confluence page, or a whole page tree, into clean Markdown for AI assistants, documentation repositories, and search pipelines.

This app is in development and not yet available on the Atlassian Marketplace.

See a sample export →

Export a page or a page tree

  1. Once available, ask your Confluence administrator to install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace and start a trial or subscription.
  2. Open a Confluence page, open its more-actions menu (•••), and choose Apps, then Export to Markdown.
  3. Choose This page or This page and all its descendants. Images are always included; tick Include other attachments to add PDFs, spreadsheets, and other files.
  4. Click Export. Progress shows each step, and Cancel stops the export at any time.
  5. When it finishes, review the summary and any warnings, then click Download.

What is in the ZIP

The file is named after the space and the page, for example ENG-engineering-handbook-2026-10-10.zip. Inside one top folder:

  • Each page is a Markdown file. Its child pages sit in a folder with the same name, and its images and attachments in a .assets folder beside it.
  • Every page starts with front matter: title, page ID, source URL, space key, parent, breadcrumb, labels, author, last editor, created and updated dates, version, and export time.
  • Links between exported pages become relative links, so they keep working in your editor, repository, or tool.
  • manifest.json lists every page with its path and metadata, plus anything skipped and why.
  • all-pages.md holds every page in tree order in a single file, ready to hand to an AI assistant.

How content converts

Output is GitHub Flavored Markdown. Headings, lists, task lists, code blocks, quotes, and tables convert directly. Panels become callouts, and tables with merged cells or lists inside cells are kept as HTML tables. Smart links become links, and mentions, dates, and status labels become readable text. Jira issue macros link to the issue. Macros the app cannot convert leave a marker in the file and a warning in the summary, so nothing disappears silently.

Limits and troubleshooting

  • Up to 500 pages per export. For a larger tree, export a smaller section.
  • Files over 25 MB, and attachments beyond 300 MB in total, are left out and listed in manifest.json; the page links to them in Confluence instead.
  • Folders become directories. Whiteboards, databases, and other content that is not a page are listed as skipped.
  • Pages you cannot view in Confluence are not exported.
  • A missing or inactive license shows a notice instead of the export options. Ask your administrator to start a trial or renew.

Privacy and support

The export runs in your browser and reads Confluence as you, with your permissions. The app has no backend or app storage, makes no external requests, and BlueConfetti never receives your pages or the exported file. Read the privacy policy or contact support.
