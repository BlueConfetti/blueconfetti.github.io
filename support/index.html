HERE TO HELP

Support

Questions, bugs, or feature ideas? Email support@blueconfetti.net.

Public issue tracker

You can also report bugs and request features on our public issue tracker, where existing reports are visible. Issues are public, so leave out confidential page content and personal data, and email us for anything private.

What to include

  • The app name and a short description of the problem.
  • Steps to reproduce it and what you expected to happen.
  • Your browser and whether the problem affects one page or several.
  • A screenshot, or a small example table or page, with private information removed.

Service level agreement

Support is provided by the developer by email, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. America/Phoenix (UTC−7).

We aim to send an initial response within two business days. This is a best-effort target; response and resolution times are not guaranteed. We do not offer round-the-clock support.

Security concerns

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to security@blueconfetti.net. Please do not include credentials or confidential page content.

Billing

Atlassian manages Marketplace purchases, subscriptions, and invoices. For an app question, contact us; for a payment or invoice problem, contact Atlassian billing support.

Before you write

The Table Glide guide explains setup, filters, sorting, pagination, and license notices. The Flint Markdown Export guide covers page selection, previews, presets, background jobs, history, ZIP contents and limits.
