FLINT MARKDOWN EXPORT · USE CASE

Create a portable onboarding handbook or project handoff

Give a new team, contractor or partner a portable copy of the pages they need, with the files, the date and the boundaries spelled out.

Finished result

A ZIP with four onboarding pages, a site map image and a safety-briefing PDF beside the page that uses them, plus a short note that states what the snapshot covers and what stays in Confluence.

Example: an operations onboarding branch

The fictional Operations onboarding page (6001) has three children: Your first day (6002), with a site map image and an attached safety briefing; Tools and access (6003); and Who to call (6004). It links to an Expense policy that should remain in Confluence.

Export the branch

  1. Open Operations onboarding and choose Apps → Export to Markdown, then This page and descendants. Only this branch is collected, not the rest of the space.
  2. Choose Standard Markdown. Title-based filenames such as your-first-day.md are easy for people to browse.
  3. Under Output details, turn on Include other attachments. Images are always included; this setting adds the PDF.
  4. Choose Review selection. The summary line reads 4 pages · Standard Markdown · Images and other attachments · Frontmatter on. Choose Prepare full preview.
  5. Inspect the links. The three child-page links became relative links; the expense policy still opens Confluence. Check the summary for warnings; the sample has none.
  6. Choose Build ZIP from preview → Download ZIP.

Package it as a dated snapshot

The archive name already includes the export date: OPS-operations-onboarding-2026-11-03.zip. Add a short note of your own beside the ZIP so the recipient knows what they have:

  • The export time from manifest.json and the source page link.
  • The pages and files included, and anything deliberately left out.
  • That Confluence remains the source of truth, and who to tell about corrections.

Before you send it

  • Check who may read it. The files do not keep Confluence permissions. Mentions become people’s display names, and frontmatter can include authors and editors; remove fields under Advanced settings if the recipient should not have them.
  • Expect it to go stale. The copy does not update. Send a new snapshot when the pages change.
  • Keep the folders together. Moving a page file without its .assets folder breaks its images and attachment links.
  • Check the size limits. Files over 75 MiB, or attachments beyond 300 MiB for the export, are left out with a message. See images and attachments.
