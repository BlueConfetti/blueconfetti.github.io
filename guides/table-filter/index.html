About the examples

All sample tables are fictional. Expected rows, counts and totals were calculated with the same filtering logic that Flint Table Filter 2.5.0 runs in the reader’s browser, and the downloadable CSV and XLSX examples were produced by the release’s export logic outside Confluence. The guides describe released behavior; screenshots are not included. Relative dates assume Monday 9 November 2026 as today.
