FLINT MARKDOWN EXPORT · HOW-TO

Find and manage export history as a user or Confluence administrator

Find out who exported what, with which settings, and clear the records you no longer need.

Finished result

You can find a past export by page, space or exporter, read its settings and warning count, and delete entries, knowing what history does and does not keep.

Open your history

  1. In the export window, choose History in the header. The Export history view lists your exports, newest first.
  2. Each row shows Date / exporter, Space / scope, Pages / files with the archive size, and Details.
  3. Type in Search loaded history to filter by page title, page ID, space, exporter or source. The count line reads, for example, 3 matching entries from 20 loaded. Load more to search older entries.
  4. Search only covers loaded entries. Choose Load more to fetch the next 20, then search again.

Read an entry’s details

Expand Details. The summary shows the warning count and the source. An entry for the handbook export in the Git guide would contain:

DetailExample
Summary1 warning · browser-reported
RootRoot: 1001. ZIP export.
SettingsMarkdown links · blockquote panels · Front matter on · Other attachments excluded
Filename{id}
MetadataThe frontmatter field names, and the names (not values) of custom fields
PagesEngineering Handbook (1001) · ENG, and the other four pages

This table is illustrative, assembled from the fields history records; it is not a screenshot.

Exports built in the browser are labeled browser-reported; background exports are server-generated. A browser export is recorded when the ZIP is built, not when you only prepare a preview. If recording fails, the summary says The export is ready, but its history entry could not be saved.

What history keeps

  • Kept: your account and display name, time, scope and root, page titles and IDs, space, output settings, counts, archive size, warning count and source.
  • Not kept: page content, attachment files, custom metadata values or the ZIP. History is activity metadata, not an archive and not a complete audit log.
  • Shortened: very long titles and space names can be shortened in history; Long titles or space keys are shortened in history. The export retains the full values.
  • Older entries: some may say Settings were not recorded for this older export.

Delete entries

  • Delete entry removes one row.
  • Clear my history asks you to confirm: Delete your history entries, including entries not loaded here? Profiles and downloaded files are kept. This cannot be undone. Choose Confirm clear history or Keep history.

For Confluence administrators

Administrators also see Show exports from all users (administrator). Turning it on loads everyone’s entries, which an administrator can inspect and delete. The clear button then reads Clear all users’ history. Other users do not see this control and only ever see their own entries.

Retention

Entries become unavailable after 90 days and are removed by scheduled cleanup. To keep a longer record of an export, keep its manifest.json with your own records; see checking a snapshot. Saved profiles are separate and remain until deleted.

This guide describes the released history view. It does not include screenshots or downloadable history, because entries belong to each site and person.
