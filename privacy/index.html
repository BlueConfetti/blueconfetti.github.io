Privacy policy

This policy covers BlueConfetti’s apps for Atlassian Cloud (Table Glide and Flint Markdown Export), this website, and our support email.

Table Glide

The macro processes the table content Confluence supplies inside the viewer’s browser. It uses that content to search, filter, sort, and paginate the displayed table. It does not change the stored page.

A stateless Forge export function returns the complete saved macro body inside Atlassian for exports. The app has no app storage, external API calls, or requested permission scopes. BlueConfetti does not receive or retain your table content. Search terms, selected filters, sorting, and the current page are temporary view state and reset when the macro reloads.

Confluence provides app license information so the macro can determine whether to enable interactive controls. The app does not send that information to BlueConfetti.

Flint Markdown Export

Flint Markdown Export is in development and is not yet available on the Atlassian Marketplace. It reads selected pages, page hierarchy, labels, attachments, space information and author/editor display names through Confluence APIs, acting with the requesting person’s permissions. The app uses read-only content permissions and Atlassian-hosted storage for the features below.

Browser and background exports

Browser exports hold prepared content in memory and build the ZIP on the user’s device. Background exports use Atlassian Forge functions as the requesting user and temporarily store their ZIP in Forge Object Store. Job records include the requester, selected page IDs, output options, progress and results. Jobs and ZIP downloads are available for one hour after creation; expiry, cancellation and scheduled cleanup limit availability. Atlassian’s hosted-storage soft-deletion and backup lifecycle applies, so one-hour availability does not mean immediate physical erasure.

Downloads check job ownership, expiry, cancellation and current visibility of exported pages and pages inlined from outside the export by include macros. Confluence-rendered macro output can contain data from other sources not covered by that page check. Download links can grant access until they expire or are deleted. An exported file already saved or shared cannot be revoked by the app and does not retain Confluence access controls.

History and profiles

Forge storage keeps export activity metadata: authenticated account ID and display name, time, scope/root, page IDs and titles, space, output settings, counts, archive size, warnings and export source. History does not store page bodies, attachment contents or custom metadata values. Long titles and space names may be shortened. Users can view and delete their own history; Confluence administrators can inspect and explicitly clear all users’ entries. History is activity metadata, not a complete audit log. Entries become unavailable after 90 days and are removed by scheduled cleanup.

Saved output profiles are per user and persist until deleted. They store settings and custom metadata values, with up to 20 profiles per user. Treat custom fields as stored data and avoid putting secrets in them.

Processing and access

No external processing backend, analytics or AI service is configured. BlueConfetti does not receive page content or exported ZIPs through the app. App logs exclude page content, credentials, signed download URLs and request bodies; background failure logs contain error name, code and HTTP status. Atlassian platform records and policies still apply.

Confluence supplies license information, and the frontend and backend require an active license for exports. Once downloaded, files are controlled by the person who saved them and your organization’s sharing, retention and deletion policies.

Atlassian services

Atlassian hosts the app and manages accounts, subscriptions, Marketplace transactions, and its platform records. Those services are governed by Atlassian’s own privacy policy and your agreement with Atlassian. Page content remains subject to your Confluence permissions and your organization’s policies.

This website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, forms, or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to operate and protect its hosting service.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request, investigate problems, and maintain support records. Email is handled through Google Workspace. Do not send passwords, API tokens, or confidential page data.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve requests and maintain relevant business records. We do not sell your information or use support messages for advertising.

Your requests

Contact support@blueconfetti.net to ask about information you have provided or request deletion, subject to records we must retain. We cannot delete or alter information held in your Confluence instance; contact your site administrator for that.
